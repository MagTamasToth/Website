Data Protection Officer

No data protection officer has been appointed, as there is currently no statutory obligation to do so based on the scope and nature of processing activities.

Provision of data

The provision of personal data in the contact form is voluntary. Without the information marked as mandatory, the enquiry cannot be processed or can only be processed to a limited extent. Alternatively, contact can be made by email or telephone.

2. Collection and storage of personal data

a) When visiting the website

When you access the website, your browser automatically sends information to the server (IP address, date and time, file requested, referrer URL, browser, operating system) which is temporarily stored in a logfile to ensure smooth operation. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest).

Hosting: This website is delivered via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Details: docs.github.com/site-policy.

b) When using the contact form

When using the contact form, the data entered (name, company, email, phone, message etc.) is processed to handle your enquiry. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (contract initiation) and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering).

The form is transmitted via the external service provider FormSubmit (FormSubmit.co), which forwards the enquiry by email to the controller. Since FormSubmit is an external provider and processing outside the EU/EEA cannot be excluded, users are expressly informed that use of the form may involve a transfer to a third country. The form is used only after active acknowledgement of this Privacy Policy. Alternatively, contact can be made by email or telephone.

You may object to processing at any time and instead contact us directly by email or phone.

c) Storage period

Enquiries are stored until the purpose of processing has ceased (typically after completion of the business transaction or after 6 months of inactivity), at most up to the statutory retention periods (e.g. § 132 BAO: 7 years for tax-relevant documents).

3. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies, marketing cookies or any web analytics tool. Only technically necessary storage mechanisms such as sessionStorage are used for language selection and the cookie notice. These values are not used for analytics.

4. Embedded content

This website does not embed third-party content such as YouTube videos, Google Maps, social media plugins, Google Analytics or marketing pixels. Fonts are loaded locally or via system fallbacks; no Google Fonts request is made to Google servers.

Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools: pure site-verification files or meta tags may be used. They serve only to confirm site ownership and monitor indexing and do not set tracking cookies on this website.

5. Recipients of the data

Your personal data is shared only with the following processors:

  • GitHub, Inc. (website hosting) — Data Processing Agreement: GitHub DPA
  • FormSubmit.co (form transmission after active acknowledgement of the Privacy Policy; email or phone contact is available as an alternative)
  • Email provider for processing of incoming emails

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to information (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21), withdrawal of a granted consent (Art. 7 (3)) with effect for the future, and to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority (Art. 77). Exercising these rights is free of charge. Where consent is requested, it is given voluntarily; refusal has no negative consequences.

Requests: tamas.toth@metallagentur.at

We respond to requests pursuant to Art. 12 (3) GDPR within one month of receipt. Where requests are complex, the period may be extended by a further two months (with notification within one month).

Automated decision-making

Automated decision-making, including profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR, does not take place.
