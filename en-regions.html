Regions

Vienna, Eastern Austria and Hungary.

Sales starts on the ground. My action radius is clearly defined — along the Vienna–Eastern Austria–Hungary axis — supplemented by selective mobility in the wider DACH region.

Region 01

Vienna

Home base and daily working environment. Vienna is the Central European headquarters city for many international brands, and at the same time the hub for market entries toward Hungary, Slovakia and the Czech Republic.

Region 02

Lower Austria

The industrial belt surrounding Vienna, with strong clusters in Wiener Neustadt, Krems and the Industrieviertel. Own mobility, regular appointments, familiar economic environment.

Region 03

Burgenland

Directly on the Hungarian border, culturally and economically intertwined with Western Hungary. Ideal for cross-border business models.

Region 04

Upper Austria & Styria

Industrial focal points in Linz, Steyr, Graz and Upper Styria. Selective project work, especially for clients with cross-regional sales needs.

Region 05

Hungary

Native language, university connections, years of sales and coordination practice in the Hungarian market — from Budapest via Veszprém and Székesfehérvár to rural regions.

Bridge function

Bridge between Austria and Hungary.

Austria and Hungary are economically closely intertwined, but linguistically, culturally and administratively independent. Anyone who seriously works both markets benefits from a mediator who is at home in both — at the table, on the phone, in the contract.
