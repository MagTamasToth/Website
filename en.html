Frequently asked questions

What people often ask before an introductory call.

How does working with a commercial agent actually work?
You contract me to mediate or conclude business in your name. You retain full control over prices, terms and contracts — I am the local sales partner.
Which industries do you really understand?
Focus areas: industry, construction and aluminium profiles, FMCG, organic food, renewable energy, cleaning and cosmetics, print and promotional materials, and recycled products. Other industries we examine together.
Do you work on a fee or commission basis?
Both are possible. A small fixed retainer to cover operational effort plus a performance-based commission is common. The exact structure is agreed individually.
Which regions are you active in?
Focus on Vienna, Lower Austria, Burgenland, Styria and Hungary. Upper Austria and the wider DACH region depending on the project.
