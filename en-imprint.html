Notice on the Finanztippgeber activity

Under trade licence GISA 38742828, no advisory services are provided regarding wealth-building, financing, investment, provision or insurance. The activity is strictly limited to referring interested persons to authorised professional asset advisors. There is no permanent mandate by the same client. Any activity reserved for professional asset advisors is excluded.
